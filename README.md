## What it does

Audit Readiness Copilot turns your evidence and control data into a live readiness score: which controls are audit-proof, which have gaps, and exactly what to fix before the auditor arrives.

## Key features

- **Readiness score** — per framework, per control, always current.
- **Gap remediation plans** — prioritized fixes with owners and dates.
- **Auditor Q&A prep** — likely questions with evidence-linked answers.
- **Mock audit runs** — AI auditor probes your evidence before the real one.
- **Timeline tracker** — milestones from kickoff to report delivery.

## Who it's for

Teams facing SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, or customer audits who want zero surprises.
